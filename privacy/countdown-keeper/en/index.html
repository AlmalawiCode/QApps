Privacy Policy for Countdown Keeper

Last updated: 27 November 2026

Countdown Keeper (“the app”) is a mobile app published by Thiban Tech Solutions. This policy explains how the app handles your information. Most of the app works only on your device, with no account. The one exception is the optional Meeting Planner tool: it needs a server to collect people's replies, and it is described in its own section below. We do not sell your information, show ads, or track you.

What stays on your device

Events and schedules with their dates and times, notes, colours, pictures you attach, a place you add to an event, reminders and alarms, and your settings are stored only on your device and are never sent to us. Export, share and encrypted backup files are created only when you ask, and you choose where they go and who gets them.

Device permissions

  • Notifications: for reminders and alarms, and for organizers' alerts in Meeting Planner.
  • Alarms and full-screen (Android): so an alarm rings on time, even when the phone is locked.
  • Camera and photos: optional, to attach a picture to an event or save one to your gallery.
  • Location: optional, only when you tap “My current location” for an event. It stays on your device.
  • Fingerprint / Face ID: optional, for app lock.
  • Run after restart (Android): to keep scheduled reminders.

Each permission is requested only when you use the feature that needs it.

Meeting Planner (server)

When you use this tool, the following is stored on our server in Google Firebase (data centres in the European Union):

  • An automatic anonymous identifier for the device, with no name or phone number.
  • Meeting content: the title, note, place and proposed times, as the organizer writes them.
  • Your reply: the name you type and the times you chose or marked.
  • For alerts: when you open a meeting someone else organizes, your name and your device's push token are stored so the organizer's alert can reach you. The alert text is kept with the meeting so you can read it in the app.

The organizer sees who replied and their choices; other participants see them too unless the organizer hides names. We never ask for your phone number or contacts. Retention: a meeting and everything about it is deleted automatically 30 days after its last time, or immediately when the organizer deletes it. You can withdraw your reply at any time.

Organizer account and points

To organize a meeting you sign in with Apple or Google and buy a points pack from the App Store or Google Play. We then keep:

  • The account identifier and email as Apple or Google provides them (Apple may provide a hidden relay email). Used only to keep your points, never for marketing.
  • Your balance and its history: purchases, points set aside for each meeting, used and returned.
  • Purchase records: store, transaction number, pack, points and date, so we can verify the purchase with Apple or Google and never count it twice.

Payment happens at Apple or Google; we never see your card details. If the store refunds a purchase, its points are removed.

Deleting your account and data

In the app: My points → ⋮ → Delete account. This deletes your account, balance, history and the meetings you organize. Only the purchase transaction record is kept, to prevent it from being credited again and for accounting. Your replies in other people's meetings are deleted with those meetings. You can also ask for deletion by email (below).

Third-party services

  • Google Firebase: the Meeting Planner server, sign-in, notifications, and checking that requests come from the genuine app (App Check with Play Integrity / DeviceCheck).
  • Apple and Google: sign-in and in-app purchases.
  • Google Fonts: downloading the display font the first time.

The server logs how many operations each request used, with the account and meeting identifiers, to calculate cost and prevent abuse. These logs are deleted automatically within 30 days. We use no advertising or tracking analytics.

Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Security

Connections to the server are encrypted. No phone can read the database directly: every read and write goes through the server, which checks that the request is allowed. On your device, data is protected by the device's own security and the optional app lock.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. Changes are posted here with a new date, and important changes are announced in the app.
