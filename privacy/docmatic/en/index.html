Privacy Policy for DocMatic

Last updated: October 2026

DocMatic (“the app”) is a mobile app published by Q Apps. It turns photos, scans and PDFs into clean PDF files. This policy explains how the app handles your information. In short: the app works on your phone, needs no account, and we do not collect, sell or share your personal information or your documents.

Information the app handles

The app handles the files you bring into it — photos, scans, screenshots and PDFs you share into DocMatic, open from Files or Photos, or take with the camera — and what you create from them: PDF files, page edits, signatures, stamps, QR codes and their history, folders and settings. To make picture pages searchable, the app reads the text on them on your phone; that text is saved inside your PDF and is never sent anywhere.

Device permissions

  • Camera — to scan documents, read QR codes and barcodes, and take ID or passport photos. Asked the first time you scan.
  • Photos and files — only through the system picker when you choose «Photos to PDF» or «Open PDF», a signature photo, or a portrait; and to save images to your gallery when you ask.
  • Contacts — optional, only when you choose a contact to put into a QR code or to add a scanned contact to your phone.
  • Location — optional, only when you choose to put your current location into a QR code or a message.
  • Wi-Fi state — optional, to fill in the current network name when you create a Wi-Fi QR code.
  • Fingerprint / Face ID — optional, only if you turn on the app lock.

Each permission is requested only when you use the related feature, and the app works without the optional ones.

Data storage

All of your files, pages, folders and settings are stored inside the app on your device. Files in a locked folder are encrypted with AES-256; they cannot be opened without your password, and there is no way for us or anyone to recover it.

Data sharing

We do not sell or share your data. The app has no user accounts and no DocMatic server; your documents are not uploaded to us. A file leaves the app only when you share it (for example to WhatsApp, email or Drive) or print it, and then it goes only to the app or printer you choose.

Third-party services

The app does not use analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting services. Opening a link, a map or a WhatsApp chat from the app hands it to that app or website, whose own privacy policy applies. If paid features are offered, payments are handled entirely by Google Play or the App Store; we never receive your payment details.

Data retention

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. Deleted files go to the app's trash and are removed for good after 30 days, or sooner if you empty it. Uninstalling the app removes all of its data.

Children's privacy

The app is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect information from children.

Security

Your data is protected by your device's security and the app's sandbox. You can add locked folders with real encryption and an optional fingerprint or Face ID app lock.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. If a future feature ever needs to send a file off your phone, it will ask for your clear consent first, and this policy will be updated before that feature is released. Changes are posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, contact us at thibantechsolutions@gmail.com.
